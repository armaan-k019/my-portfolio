The Shape Computation Lab at Georgia Tech develops computational tools that bridge architectural design and algorithmic reasoning. The Shape Machine is a vector-based system for identifying and modifying shapes across CAD files, supporting iterative and generative design workflows through computational methods applied to geometric primitives. The full lab team is actively developing further publications around the Shape Machine and its applications in computational design.
