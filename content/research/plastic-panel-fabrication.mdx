This research, conducted under Professor Hyojin Kwon at Georgia Tech, investigates the material properties and fabrication processes involved in creating plastic panels from waste collected across the Georgia Tech campus. The work sits at the intersection of material science and architectural design, asking how institutional waste streams can be transformed into building components with civic purpose. The research contributes to a broader inquiry into campus waste as a design resource, connecting material recovery directly to questions of spatial justice and sustainable construction.
