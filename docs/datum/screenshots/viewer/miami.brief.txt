Site brief
CLAUDE, CLAUDE-SONNET-4-6
GROUND
The site sits at latitude 25.80116 and longitude -80.18906 [site.latitude, site.longitude]. Elevation is 2 m above sea level [topo.siteElevationM]. The terrain is nearly flat: mean slope is 0.7 percent, and total relief across the survey grid is 4.4 m [topo.meanSlopePct, topo.reliefM]. East-west cross sections run between 0.1 m and 3.8 m, north-south between 1.7 m and 3.2 m [topo.sections.ew[], topo.sections.ns[]]. At 2 m elevation with flat, low-permeability soils, finished floor elevation and stormwater retention are primary design drivers. The dominant soil component is Urban land at 85 percent of the map unit [soil.components[].percent]. The remaining named components are all rated poorly drained, and two of four ranked components carry a hydric designation [soil.components[].drainageClass, soil.components[].hydric]. Do not assume good infiltration: design for detained or pumped stormwater.
CLIMATE AND SUN
Cooling degree days total 2454 against a base of 18.3 C, while heating degree days are only 36 [climate.degreeDays.cdd, climate.degreeDays.hdd, climate.degreeDays.baseC]. Only 13.3 percent of hours fall in the simple comfort band [climate.comfortShare.pct]. The envelope must prioritize cooling above all else. Peak solar noon altitude reaches 87.6 deg in June, dropping to 40.8 deg in December [sun.june.noonAltitudeDeg, sun.december.noonAltitudeDeg]. The calculated overhang ratio for south glazing is 0.04, meaning almost no overhang is needed there [sun.overhangRatioSouthGlazing]. East and west glazing carry far greater solar load given the near-vertical summer sun. Annual prevailing wind is from 90 deg (east) at a mean of 3.33 m/s [climate.wind.annual.prevailingSectorDeg, climate.wind.annual.meanSpeedMs]. Summer winds shift to 135 deg at a mean of 2.65 m/s [climate.wind.summer.prevailingSectorDeg, climate.wind.summer.meanSpeedMs]. Orient operable openings to capture southeast flow for passive cooling.

RISK
The site point falls in FEMA Zone X, rated moderate hazard at the 0.2 percent annual chance flood level [flood.atPoint.zone, flood.atPoint.class, flood.atPoint.subtype]. Adjacent polygons include Zone AE and Zone VE, both Special Flood Hazard Areas [flood.polygons[].zone, flood.polygons[].sfha]. Combined with 2 m site elevation and poorly drained soils, ground-floor program and mechanical equipment should be elevated. Seismic design category is A under ASCE 7-22, with a peak ground acceleration of 0.025 g [seismic.sdc, seismic.pgam, seismic.assumptions.reference]. Seismic demands are negligible; wind and flood govern structural decisions.
